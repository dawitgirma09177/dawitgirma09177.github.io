About Me

I am a Lecturer and Researcher at Wolkite University, Ethiopia, with an academic background in Hydraulic and Water Resources Engineering and Civil Engineering (Hydraulic Engineering). My academic and professional interests focus on hydrology, water resources planning and management, hydraulic engineering, groundwater, irrigation and drainage, flood risk management, GIS, remote sensing and the application of artificial intelligence in water resources engineering.

I also have practical engineering experience in water supply and irrigation projects, including engineering documentation, design review, construction supervision, quantity surveying and project coordination.
