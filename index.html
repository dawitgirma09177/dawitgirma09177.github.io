About Me

I am a Lecturer and Researcher at Wolkite University, Ethiopia, with academic qualifications in Hydraulic and Water Resources Engineering and Civil Engineering (Hydraulic Engineering). I have been serving in higher education since September 2017.

My academic and professional interests focus on hydrology, hydrological modeling, water resources planning and management, groundwater assessment, flood risk management, irrigation engineering, and the application of geospatial technologies in water resources studies.

Alongside my academic career, I have gained practical engineering experience in water supply, irrigation, and road construction projects, including engineering design documentation, site supervision, quality control, and construction management.
