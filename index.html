About Me

I am Dawit Girma Buayu, a Lecturer and Researcher at Wolkite University, Ethiopia, specializing in Water Resources Engineering. I hold an M.Sc. degree in Civil Engineering (Hydraulic Engineering) from Addis Ababa University and a B.Sc. degree in Hydraulic and Water Resources Engineering from Wollega University.

My academic and professional experience includes university teaching, scientific research, engineering design, water supply projects and irrigation development.

My research focuses on sustainable water resources development, hydrological modeling, climate resilience, groundwater assessment, flood risk management and geospatial technologies.
